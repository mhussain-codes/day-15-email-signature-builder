# Signoff Studio — Day 15

Build a clean email signature from your contact details. Preview it as you edit, then copy a formatted HTML signature or a plain-text version for your email app.

**Live demo:** https://mhussain-codes.github.io/day-15-email-signature-builder/

## Features

- Live preview with Classic, Modern, and Minimal styles.
- Add name, role, company, email, phone, website, LinkedIn, and location.
- Choose an accent color.
- Copy a formatted signature for pasting into an email client.
- Copy plain text or download the email-safe HTML.
- Table-based layout and inline styles for broad email-client compatibility.
- No account, tracking, uploads, or third-party libraries.

## Use it

1. Open the [live demo](https://mhussain-codes.github.io/day-15-email-signature-builder/).
2. Replace the sample details with your own.
3. Choose a style and accent color.
4. Select **Copy formatted signature** and paste it into your mail app’s signature settings.
5. Send a test email to yourself; email apps can render signatures differently.

The **View HTML source** section lets you inspect or copy the markup. Use **Copy plain text** if your mail app does not accept formatted pastes.

## Privacy and safety

The fields are processed in this browser tab only. Signoff Studio does not send or store the details you enter. Use contact information that you are comfortable including in email.

## Run locally

Open index.html in a browser. The project has no build step or third-party dependencies.
